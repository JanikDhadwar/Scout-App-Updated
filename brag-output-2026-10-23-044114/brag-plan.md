# Argus — See it. Scout it. Select.

## Planning rubric
1. Argus collects FRC match/pit evidence and coordinates scouts, then supports alliance decisions.
2. Strongest moment: a real scoring play becomes a saved scouting observation and then usable strategy evidence.
3. Hook: the actual homepage fans across a laptop, tablet and phone.
4. Product material: actual local app captures, enlarged live-action HTML controls and the user-supplied robot footage.
5. Duration: 45 seconds. This expanded feature tour intentionally exceeds brag's 15–25-second default to satisfy the request for more features without unreadable rapid copy.
6. Tone: cinematic with energetic app-store pacing; quick cuts, intentional punch-ins, interactive controls, confident red actions. No slow idle drift.
7. Audio: upbeat bundled vol-1 music at 0.35, restrained action clicks/soft impacts, original match sound quietly under the four-second clip. No narration.
8. Caption: See the play. Capture the evidence. Make the next pick. Argus brings match scouting, pit visits, coordinated duties and alliance strategy to laptop, tablet and phone.
9. Flow: homepage → real play → scout entry → coordinated crew → pit/forms → analytics → picklist/export → all-device finish.
