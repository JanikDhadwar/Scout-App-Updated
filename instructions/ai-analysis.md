# AI scouting analysis

Open Scouting analyst directly, or use the analyst within Analytics. Start with a suggested question, a named team or a configured metric.

Local calculations work without Groq. An Owner can configure Groq for optional interpretations. Provider errors and quota limits fall back to local answers.

Check source reports, sample sizes, coverage, disagreement and missing/invalid counts. Pit evidence is separate from match averages. Metrics from different forms/units are not combined into invented scores.

Scouters use Admin-approved shared measurements. Other raw reports, private notes, photos and scout names are not sent to the provider. Questions do not assume memory of earlier answers: name the teams or metrics in each follow-up.

AI interpretations may be wrong. Unsupported scoring rules, capabilities or predictions should be treated as insufficient information.
