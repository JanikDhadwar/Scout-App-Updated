# Alliance strategy

Open Strategy, choose or create an event picklist, and add teams from the directory. Only Admins/Owners edit shared lists; Scouters can inspect permitted data.

Use two to four selected teams for comparison. Review the same metric and units, sample size, missing data, disagreement and scouting coverage.

Move entries with the up/down buttons on touch or keyboard; drag is optional. Save to confirm the list on the server. Unsaved list drafts are preserved locally. A conflicting revision requires reviewing the saved version; it must not silently replace your edits.

Strategy notes and Needs review markers record your judgement. Pick order is not official ranking and is not a predicted match outcome.
