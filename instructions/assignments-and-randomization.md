# Assignments and randomization

A permanent owner receives a list of robot teams for the entire event. That scout pit scouts those teams and match scouts each scheduled appearance.

1. An Admin opens Assignments → Manage ownership.
2. Select participating scouts and the pit/match forms.
3. Choose Fill unowned teams to retain saved owners, or explicitly choose Rerandomize unlocked teams.
4. Preview team counts, allocations and collision warnings.
5. Apply the preview. A stale preview is rejected without overwriting newer work.

Team counts are balanced when unconstrained. Locked or preserved ownership can limit balance. Collision minimization is a heuristic and cannot guarantee zero conflicts.

Use Move or lock individual teams for permanent changes. Accepted handoffs and explicit match overrides are preserved. Deselection prevents new ownership; it does not silently abandon existing teams. Move those teams explicitly.

My work shows permanent teams, pit progress, next duties, conflicts and coverage. Pending upload is not server-confirmed completion. Schedule refresh reconciles new duties without duplicating them.
