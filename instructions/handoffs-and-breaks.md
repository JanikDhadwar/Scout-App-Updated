# Handoffs and breaks

Use Request cover on a match assignment, or open that match and choose Handoff for the robot.

Select a connected teammate who has no robot in that match. You remain responsible until they accept. They can decline; you can cancel a pending offer.

Offers expire after five minutes or at the scheduled/predicted match start, whichever is sooner. Handoffs close when the match starts. Unknown start times do not permit a handoff. Both peers must be connected when required; push delivery is not acceptance.

Acceptance changes one robot in one match only. Permanent team ownership and other matches remain unchanged. A confirmed matching report completes the handoff. Admins can inspect pending, accepted, declined, cancelled, expired and completed history.

For a short break covering multiple matches, request each required match explicitly; there is no silent event-wide transfer.
