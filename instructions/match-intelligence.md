# Match intelligence

Open Pit display and choose an upcoming match. The match page shows the three opposing teams relative to your configured display team. Back to Pit Display returns to the stream and upcoming schedule.

## Strengths and concerns

Each opponent has up to three evidence-supported strengths and concerns, plus last-match observations. At least three distinct usable observed matches are required for a strength or concern. Missing observations never become weaknesses. Unknown and not-attempted outcomes do not count as failures.

## Evidence confidence

Starting evidence labels are Preliminary for one or two usable matches, Limited for three or four, Moderate for five to seven, and Strong for eight or more. More than 20% missing or invalid eligible match answers, unresolved disagreements, and at least two later completed matches without a usable observation each reduce the label by one band. Reductions stop at Preliminary. Subjective evidence and unknown schedules cap confidence at Moderate.

Expand evidence to see counts, reasons, a trend and its data table, retrieval context, and source report links. Your report permissions still apply. Confidence describes evidence quality rather than the probability that a robot will succeed.

## Last-match comparisons

The latest recorded match follows official schedule order rather than upload time. Comparisons show up to three preceding observed matches and the full-event baseline. Small comparisons are preliminary. Phase totals or season points are unavailable unless explicitly configured; observations are not official robot scores.

## Match13 estimates

The separately labelled strip shows available scores and alliance win probability from Match13. These are estimates, not results. Cached forecasts show their retrieval time and become stale after two minutes. A schedule change can invalidate a cached forecast. Participant sets cannot independently verify a provider's alliance-color assignment. No Argus win probability is calculated.

## Offline and permissions

Downloaded authorized evidence remains available offline. New uploads stay pending until confirmed. If no approved evidence is available, ask an admin to configure and share measurements. No AI service is required.
