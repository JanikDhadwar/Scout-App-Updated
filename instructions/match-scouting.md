# Match scouting

1. Open Assignments or the match schedule.
2. Choose a robot assigned to you in that match.
3. Confirm the event, team, match, alliance and form.
4. Record observed answers. Missing information is not zero.
5. Submit, then check upload status.

You can scout only your assigned robot or accepted coverage. If two owned teams play simultaneously, request one-match cover before the match starts. An Admin can review unresolved coverage.

Match keys identify playoff sets as well as match numbers. Do not manually reuse another match's draft context. Saved drafts with different context are preserved and show a warning.
