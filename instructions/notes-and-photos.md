# Notes and robot photos

Pit and match forms already support notes and robot photos. Open a team profile for observations, pit evidence, confidence and source reports. Each observation retains its report context.

Use labelled observations rather than inventing conclusions from missing data. Capture useful robot views without unnecessary personal information. Photos are compressed on the phone and remain in the offline report queue until acknowledged.

Raw notes/photos follow report permissions. Scouter shared summaries publish structured measurements only. A separately curated shared gallery and external media migration remain a later roadmap item; do not assume private photos are public.
