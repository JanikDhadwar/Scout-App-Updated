# Pit scouting

Open My assignments and choose Pit scout for a team. Use the assigned pit form, record observations, then submit.

Draft answers save automatically on this phone. Submission first saves a local queued report; check Device and sync for server confirmation.

A pit visit marker and a pit report are different. Marking a visit does not complete a required pit report. Undoing a visit preserves its history and never deletes a report.

Photos and notes remain part of their original reports. Pit measurements are separate from match performance and do not inflate match coverage.

Report limits refresh every ten seconds while the page is visible and connected, and are checked again before saving a report. A full team is marked MAXED automatically. Simultaneous starts remain possible; the server enforces the final limit. Offline devices cannot see other scouts’ latest submissions: keep the draft and check upload status after reconnection.
