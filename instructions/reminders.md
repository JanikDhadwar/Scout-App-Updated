# Assignment reminders

Open Assignments to see next duties and conflicts. The handoff inbox displays incoming requests and status changes while Argus is open.

An upload queue is not a notification delivery guarantee. Phone sleep, lost connectivity, denied notification permission or expired requests can delay alerts. Always review assignments before a match.

Open Settings → Reminders for the in-app inbox, availability, routine reminders and optional push. Push requires HTTPS and browser permission; iPhones may require installing Argus on the Home Screen. No permission prompt appears until you choose Enable. Disabling stops this device's workspace subscription.

Routine reminders include an upcoming match, a daily incomplete-pit reminder and conflicts. Routine push is quiet from 22:00 to 07:00 in your configured timezone. Handoff status alerts remain time-sensitive. Repeated reminders are deduplicated and delivery retries are bounded. Delivery never accepts a handoff or changes responsibility. Request coverage in time for the recipient to open Argus and accept.
