# Starter forms

Beginner pit and Beginner match are optional templates. Open pit or match forms, choose Use a template, preview its questions, then copy and edit. Saving creates a separate workspace form. No existing form or submission is replaced.

## Prepare for the season

The templates do not assume a game's scoring rules. Add numeric questions for the actions your scouts can count. Set units, phases, preferred directions, and explicit strength and concern thresholds in Event statistics → Metric definitions. Template defaults can populate outcome rules for admin review. Save the definitions and approve shared measurements before members use them.

## Guidance and final review

Admins choose Quick or Guided guidance per form and may add hints, examples, and comparisons against earlier numeric answers. Beginner forms start Guided. Existing forms stay Quick. Review always appears last. A warning does not mean the scout is wrong or unreliable.

## Preserve history

Changes apply to new reports. Unfinished drafts retain their question snapshots. Archived forms remain accessible for unfinished drafts. Download saved workspace forms before going offline; copying or editing a form needs a server connection.
